## Status vocabulary

- DECIDED — directly decided by the project owner.
- EXPERIMENTAL — proposed and subject to implementation/testing.
- CANON / APPROVED — consolidated decision accepted for the project.
- DEFERRED — intentionally postponed.
- REJECTED — explicitly removed.

## Evidence rule

A reference, experiment or analogy must never silently become a product requirement. Each requirement must retain its provenance and status.
